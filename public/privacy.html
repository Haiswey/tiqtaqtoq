TiQ-TaQ-ToQ privacy

Last updated: 4 November 2026. Back to the game

Pupils

Pupils do not log in. Names typed before a game stay on the pupil's own device and are not sent to us. No answers, scores or results are collected.

Teachers

Teachers log in with Google. We receive only the teacher's email address, name and Google account ID, to keep each teacher's question sets private to them.

  • We store the question sets and the pictures a teacher uploads.
  • Anyone with a set's game code can play that set.
  • We do not sell, share or use this information for advertising.

Data is stored with Supabase (Singapore) and the site is hosted on Vercel. To delete your account and sets, contact the owner below.
